# 🌩️ StormVision AI

## Smart India Hackathon 2026

### Problem Statement
SIH26084 – Hyper-Local Nowcasting and Early Warning System for Thunderstorms, Hailstorms and Cloudbursts

### Team Name
Shot Boundary

---

## 🚨 Problem

Current weather alerts are often broad and delayed. Citizens, farmers and authorities need hyper-local warnings before dangerous weather events occur.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

StormVision AI is an AI-powered early warning platform that predicts:

- Thunderstorms
- Hailstorms
- Cloudbursts

and generates location-specific alerts for citizens, farmers and disaster management authorities.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- Hyper-Local Weather Prediction
- AI Risk Assessment
- Interactive Risk Maps
- Real-Time Alerts
- AI Disaster Assistant
- Multilingual Notifications
- Disaster Management Dashboard
